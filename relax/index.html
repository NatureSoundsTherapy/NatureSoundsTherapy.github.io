FAQ

What are the most relaxing nature sounds?
Most people settle on gentle rain, distant birdsong or calm waves. The key is authenticity: real recordings carry small one-off events that keep the sound alive without demanding attention.

Real recordings vs generated ambience — does it matter?
Yes: generated or looped tracks repeat motifs, which the brain eventually notices. A real recording never repeats itself.
