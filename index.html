We're a father-and-son duo capturing the natural sounds of Poland, the German borderlands, and beyond — forests, meadows, rivers, and mountain trails. The Karkonosze mountains, the Masurian Lakes, the Black Forest.

My son has autism. The forests, mountain trails, and the sound of running water are where he finds his greatest peace, joy, and the freedom to be himself. These trips became our shared world.

Every recording is from a specific place, captured on a specific day. No music, no narration, no artificial layers. Gear: Lumix S5, Insta360 Pro, Zoom F3, Sennheiser MKE 600, Rode NT1.

FAQ

Are the recordings real or AI-generated?
Every recording is a real outdoor field recording from a specific place on a specific day — no music, no narration, no loops and no AI. We record long continuous takes, often 7–12 hours from a single location, on a Zoom F3 (32-bit float) with a Sennheiser MKE 600 and Rode NT1.

What are the recordings for?
Sleep, relaxation and focus: 4K scenic films, black-screen versions for overnight listening, 360° VR soundscapes and podcasts — rain, rivers, birdsong, crickets, wind and forest ambience.
