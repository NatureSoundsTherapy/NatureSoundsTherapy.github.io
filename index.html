Our story

Why we record

We’re a father-and-son duo capturing the natural sounds of Poland, the German borderlands and beyond — the Karkonosze, the Masurian Lakes, the Bieszczady, the Black Forest.

My son has autism. Forests, mountain trails and running water are where he finds his greatest peace and the freedom to be himself. These trips became our shared world.

Every recording is from a specific place on a specific day. Often the microphone simply stays in the forest overnight and listens.

FAQ

Questions

Are the recordings real or AI-generated?

Every recording is a real outdoor field recording from a specific place on a specific day — no music, no narration, no loops and no AI. Long continuous takes, often 7–12 hours from a single location, on a Zoom F3 (32-bit float) with a Sennheiser MKE 600 and Rode NT1.

What are the recordings for?

Sleep, relaxation and focus: 4K scenic films, black-screen versions for overnight listening, 360° VR soundscapes and podcasts — rain, rivers, birdsong, crickets, wind and forest ambience.

Can I license these recordings for a film, game or app?

Yes — lossless source files are available for licensing. See the licensing page or reach out via any of the links on this site.
