License the Real Recordings

RAW source files for film, games, apps, installations & research

Every sound and scene on this channel exists as a source file you can license: the same real, documented outdoor recording — before platform compression. One licence, one honest file, no hidden library re-sales.

What you get

  • Audio: WAV up to 192 kHz / 32-bit float (Zoom F3 — full dynamics, no clipping), single continuous takes up to 12 hours
  • Video: 4K scenic (Lumix S5) and 8K 360° VR (Insta360 Pro), with the original camera files available
  • Provenance per file: place, date, that day's weather (temperature, precipitation, wind), sunrise/sunset, gear — documented metadata, not guesses
  • Clean guarantee: files are machine-scanned and human-checked for speech and man-made noise before delivery

Typical uses

Film & TV ambience beds · game audio (long, non-repeating environmental loops cut from real takes) · wellness & sleep apps · museum and spa installations · acoustic research. For AI/ML training datasets, see our curated collections — ask about dataset licensing.

How to license

Tell us which recording (any link from the catalogue) and the intended use — you'll get the source-file specs and a quote.

Scope: the licence is agreed per recording and per project — the quote states the permitted use (media, territory, term) and whether exclusivity is requested.

In your e-mail, please include:

  • the link to the recording (or the file name / place and date)
  • your project and intended use (film, game, app, installation, research)
  • media, territory and term you need, and whether you need exclusivity
