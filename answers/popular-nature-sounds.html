What are 10 popular nature sounds?

The ten most popular nature sounds are rain, thunderstorms, ocean waves, rivers, streams, waterfalls, birdsong, crickets, wind in trees and a crackling fire.

Rain & Thunder for Sleep | Storm Over a Quiet Lake

  1. Rain: the most searched one; steady and good for covering noise.
  2. Thunderstorm: deep and dramatic, nicer for the evening than for sleep because of sudden rumbles.
  3. Ocean waves: a slow, regular swell that many people find calming.
  4. River: continuous, full, broadband water.
  5. Stream or creek: lighter and brighter than a river, with small babbling sounds.
  6. Waterfall: dense and loud, a strong mask for noise.
  7. Birdsong: best in the morning or at dusk, lively and pleasant by day.
  8. Crickets: the sound of a warm summer evening, steady and soft.
  9. Wind in trees: a gentle rustle, often with leaves.
  10. Campfire or fireplace: a soft hiss with warm crackles.

Pick by purpose: rivers and rain for sleep and study, birdsong and waves to relax, fire for an evening. A real recording matters more than the label, because it does not repeat and has no loop point. Three of ours are below: rain with distant thunder, ocean waves and a meadow of crickets.
