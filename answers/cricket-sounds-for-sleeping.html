Cricket sounds for sleeping

Cricket sounds work for sleep because they are steady and quiet: a soft, even chirping with no sudden changes, so the brain stops paying attention to it. The best recordings for a full night are 8 hours or longer, with a black screen and no music. All recordings below are real field recordings of crickets on summer meadows and forest edges in Poland — one continuous take, no loops, no AI.

Questions

Are cricket sounds good for sleep?

For many people, yes. The sound is steady and low, it masks small household noises and does not have sudden changes that would wake you up.

Is this a real recording or AI-generated?

Real. Every recording here was made outdoors with field recorders on a specific night in Poland. No music, no AI, no loops.

How long should I play cricket sounds at night?

If you want it to run until morning, pick a recording of 8 hours or more — then it does not stop or loop in the middle of the night.
