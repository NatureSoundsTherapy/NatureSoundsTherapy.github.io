Sausages First, Recording Later — One Night at the Lake Fire

<p>The gear lives in the van, so when we got to the lake the sausages and the fun came first. The recorder stayed packed until the food was gone and the fire had settled into something worth capturing. Only then did the MKE600 come out and get set next to the camera, pointed at the flames.

It was a short trip, one night, late summer. One of the last warm nights when you can still sit out in a T-shirt, with autumn close and the evenings already getting colder. Firewood was easy to get at this spot, which helped, because a fire that size eats a lot. The kit was the Panasonic Lumix S5 for the picture, a Zoom F3 for the sound, and a Sennheiser MKE 600 on the fire.

Imperfect detail: starting late means the first stretch of the evening isn't on tape. You miss the early flames and the noise of the cooking. What you get is the fire after it calmed down, which is the part people actually fall asleep to anyway.

It's a static shot on a nearly black screen, with steady crackle and nothing sudden in it. Every download is the real recording — no music, no AI.

Thanks for keeping this independent.
