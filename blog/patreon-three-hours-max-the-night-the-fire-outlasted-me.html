Three Hours Max — The Night the Fire Outlasted Me

<p> I told myself I'd sit up with the fire for three hours, tops. That was the honest limit. I was too tired to keep watch, and I said so out loud before following the little explorer to bed. The hearth didn't care. It kept popping and hissing behind me.

This was an old house far from the nearest road, a summer night, and the hearth was the only thing running. I set up the Zoom F3 with the Sennheiser MKE 600 near the logs and fed the fire a few times early on. Dry logs popped, sparks snapped, and the wood settled into embers before fresh pieces caught. Not a perfect session. I wasn't watching the whole time, so I can't tell you every moment of it.

My son was asleep before I went to bed.

The video is a single static black frame, and the audio is all there is. No voices, no music, nothing else in the room.

Every download is the real recording — no music, no AI.
