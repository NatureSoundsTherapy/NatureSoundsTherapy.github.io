Forest Sounds & Birdsong · 2026-07-26

Spring Birdsong · Beech Forest, Western Poland for Relaxation

Layered bird calls in a beech forest in western Poland — a sunny spring afternoon, birds answering each other across the canopy, a calm river underneath.

No wind on mic, no traffic.

Recorded with a Zoom F3 and Rode NT1 near a wooden footbridge.

Good for relaxation, quiet focus, or background while you read.
