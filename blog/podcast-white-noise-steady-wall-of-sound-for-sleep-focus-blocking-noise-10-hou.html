The Colors of Sound: Noise Therapy · 2025-06-25

White Noise · Steady Wall of Sound for Sleep, Focus & Blocking Noise (10 Hours)

White noise — equal energy across all frequencies, a steady "wall of sound" close to a waterfall, an air conditioner, or old TV static.

Ten hours of it, flat and unchanging, to mask traffic, neighbours and other distractions while you sleep, work or study.

Lossless WAV/FLAC on Patreon: A clean, continuously generated sound — no music, no narration.

Follow for brown, pink, grey and green noise too.
