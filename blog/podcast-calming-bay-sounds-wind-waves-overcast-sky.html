Water Sounds for Sleep · 2025-11-02

Calming Bay Sounds | Wind, Waves & Overcast Sky

In this episode, we take you to the shore of a calm bay under an overcast sky.

Listen to the unique symphony of nature—the gentle sound of waves softly rolling onto the beach, combined with the quiet murmur of the wind.

It's a soundscape full of peace and melancholy, perfect for quieting the mind.

We created this session to give you a tool to find peace in the chaos of daily life.

This is pure, natural white noise, ideal for meditation, yoga, reading, or simply as a moment of respite and relaxation after a long day.

Recorded On / Gear Used: Audio: Sennheiser MKE 600 in a RODE BLIMP + Zoom F3 + RODE NT1 (Background) All sounds are our own original field recordings from sound expeditions.
