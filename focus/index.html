FAQ

Is white noise or nature sound better for studying?
Real water recordings behave like broadband white noise but are less fatiguing over long sessions — the natural micro-variation keeps masking effective without becoming irritating.

How loud should background sound be for focus?
Just below conversation level — loud enough to mask the room, quiet enough that you stop noticing it after a minute.
