FAQ

Which nature sound is best for falling asleep?
Steady, low-variation sounds work best: rain on leaves, a river or stream, or night crickets. Choose one long continuous recording rather than a playlist, so nothing changes abruptly while you drift off.

Are these recordings safe to leave on all night?
Yes — the overnight recordings are single continuous takes of many hours with a steady natural level: no ads inside the audio, no sudden edits, no music.
