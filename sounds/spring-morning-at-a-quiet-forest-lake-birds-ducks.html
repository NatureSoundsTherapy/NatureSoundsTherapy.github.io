Spring Morning at a Quiet Forest Lake • Birds & Ducks

A small forest lake at first light — quiet morning birdsong and duck calls across the water, songbirds moving through the trees along the shore. Cool air, no wind to speak of.

The recording follows the slow shift from early dawn into full morning. No music, no voices. Just what was there.
