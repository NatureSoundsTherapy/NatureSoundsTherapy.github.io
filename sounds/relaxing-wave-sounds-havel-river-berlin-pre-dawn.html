Relaxing Wave Sounds, Havel River, Berlin • Pre-Dawn

Relaxing wave sounds recorded at the Havel River in Berlin, early spring, before dawn. Clear and calm — just water hitting the shore, no wind, no traffic.

The city was completely empty. Bare tree branches overhead, dark water stretching flat to the far bank — and nothing moving. I couldn't sleep, so I packed sandwiches, drove through empty Berlin streets, and set up alone on the riverbank in the dark. This is what I needed — a reset on a Saturday night. Waves hitting the shore, and only that sound. In a few hours the city would start waking up. It didn't feel like that yet.

Slow, low laps against the bank. The water has a long fetch across the river — you can hear it in the rhythm. Gear: shotgun mic + Zoom H1N, positioned directly above the water surface.

No filters, no noise reduction, no AI.
