Winter Wind in the Trees for Sleep | Dry Leaves, Remote Forest

Winter wind through the trees for sleep or quiet focus — cold air moving through a remote forest on a winter morning, dry leaves rustling soft and papery across the ground, close to natural white noise.

Recorded out in the forest over two days at −22°C. Dry leaves on the floor and clinging to the lower branches make that papery winter rustle when the wind moves through — that texture is the whole recording. Shotgun aimed down at the leaves, a Rode NT1 set back off-frame for the room. Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Lumix S5. No music, no noise reduction.

Honestly, without the Webasto heater in the car there'd be no comfort out there — a good sleeping bag and a thermos got us through. Beautiful winter landscapes, though. My son sat this one out; I'm not taking him into that kind of cold.
