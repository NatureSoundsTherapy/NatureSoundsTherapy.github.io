Nature Sounds for Reading in 360 VR | Bieszczady Mountain River

Sleep timer — stops the video after:

360 VR nature sounds for reading: press play, look anywhere, and you're on a rocky riverbank in the Bieszczady mountains, sounds from nature on every side.

In summer you can walk right in the river — but after rain it turns into a monster. Three days just to see nobody — nature and nothing else. And bears. Same stretch of water the whole time, rocky banks grown over with forest closing it in on both sides. I could honestly live here.

• Gear: Insta360 Pro, Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Sennheiser MKE 600 (MKE 600 aimed at the water, NT1 as background, camera set beside the mics) • Format: 360 VR, look around freely or lock a fixed frame

Recorded together with my son. Bieszczady — we come back whenever we can.

Q: Is this real or AI-generated? A: Real field recording from the Bieszczady mountains, captured with an Insta360 Pro and a Zoom F3 running a Rode NT1 and a Sennheiser MKE 600 — no music, no AI.

Q: How should I watch 360? A: A headset puts you in the middle of the river. If you don't have one, drag the frame on desktop or move your phone around, or just park the view on the water and read.
