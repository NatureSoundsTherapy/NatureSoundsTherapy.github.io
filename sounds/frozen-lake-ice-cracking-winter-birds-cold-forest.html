Frozen Lake Ice Cracking • Winter Birds & Cold Forest

Across a frozen lake, the ice shifts on its own — short cracks, low pops, slow groans under the surface. Distant winter birds call from the treeline; the forest behind it stays cold and quiet.

The cracking is irregular: some minutes are almost silent, then the ice moves again. The background tone holds steady, so it sits well behind rest or quiet evenings. No music, no voices — just the lake and the air around it.
