Cold Wind for Relaxation • Frozen Lake in Winter, Open Ice

Cold winter wind for relaxing or quiet background — steady air moving across the open ice of a frozen forest lake, wide and even, close to natural white noise.

The lake is fully frozen, so the wind travels across the surface without anything to break it. Distant woodland sits at the edges; the sound stays minimal — wind, cold air, the low tone of an open winter landscape. Captured on location, far from roads. No music, no added layers.
