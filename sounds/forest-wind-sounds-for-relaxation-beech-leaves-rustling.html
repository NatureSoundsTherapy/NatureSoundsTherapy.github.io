Forest Wind Sounds for Relaxation • Beech Leaves Rustling

Soft wind for relaxing or reading — just the rustle of a beech forest at midday in summer, leaves moving overhead, nothing else in the mix. The forest runs for kilometers in every direction here. We came across this place once and kept coming back; I've always liked beech woods, but there's something different about this one. Set the camera on a static shot, started the recorder, and went off for a walk while it ran.

Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Sennheiser MKE 600, Lumix S5. No music, nothing added.
