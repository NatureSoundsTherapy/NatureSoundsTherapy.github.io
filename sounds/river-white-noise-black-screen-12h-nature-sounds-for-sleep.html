River White Noise Black Screen 12H — Nature Sounds for Sleep

If steady water is what puts you to sleep, this river white noise plays on a black screen for 12 hours, so the room stays dark all night. A mountain stream runs at a consistent flow that covers traffic, neighbors, and other sounds. No music, no talking, just the water.

Gear: Sennheiser MKE 600 in a RODE BLIMP, Zoom F3, RODE NT1. Our own original field recording.
