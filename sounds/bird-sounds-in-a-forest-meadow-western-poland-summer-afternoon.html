Bird Sounds in a Forest Meadow, Western Poland • Summer Afternoon

Bird sounds from a sun-lit meadow in Western Poland, recorded on a summer afternoon. Cuckoo calls, rustling leaves, insects — clean sounds of a bird-filled clearing, no wind on the mic, no traffic.

The meadow opens up fast. Tall grass, wet ground along the edges, a wall of mixed forest on every side. We drove out on a Wednesday afternoon without a plan. A small clearing showed up on the map, and we packed a hammock, the recorder, and a phone. I thought this place wouldn't work for him. I was wrong.

Bird song runs the whole recording. A cuckoo calls several times. At one point a roe deer barked from the tree line — my son looked over and said, "Dad, those are dogs!" Rustling leaves and crickets fill the rest.

Gear: GoPro 10, Rode NT1, Zoom F3 — mic at the forest edge, behind the camera, facing the clearing.

Really a shame to leave — this place looked like nothing on the map and sounded like everything. No filters, no noise reduction, no AI.
