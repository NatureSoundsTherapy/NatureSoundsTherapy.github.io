Mountain River White Noise for Sleep • 10 Hours

Steady, unbroken current that runs all night. Ten hours of raw water recorded straight through, no loops, no edits. A natural white noise for sleep that doesn't fade or shift — real water pushing through real rock.

It was another unplanned trip like always. We got to the site at dusk and the fire was already going. Camp set up, fire burning, and we're right next to this river with its sound the whole time. Shotgun mic pointed directly at the water for 10 hours straight. The river can rise over 1.5 meters in an hour after rain, so we had to account for that sleeping in a tent nearby. No drama overnight — water stayed where it was. My son was with me on this one. He sleeps like never before. Wondering what his dreams are like.

Gear: Zoom F3, Rode MKE600 — stereo, static recording, summer evening. Best with headphones.
