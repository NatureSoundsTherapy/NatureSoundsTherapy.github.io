Cricket Sounds for Sleeping, All Night by the Lake | 10 Hours

Sleep timer — stops the video after:

Cricket sounds for sleeping that don't shift or fade, ten hours straight from a lake in central Poland, no music, no cuts, no volume changes.

The crickets were loud. Wall-to-wall loud, the kind where you stop hearing individual insects and it turns into one flat sheet of sound. We drove out to a forest lake in central Poland and stayed two days. On the second night I set the NT1 microphone pair up in A/B stereo configuration pointed at the meadow beside the campfire, capturing crickets from the field while the fire burned next to me. Static camera, static mic. The moon just kept moving in front of us the whole time. My son was there for the full two-day stay at the lake. We're not fishing for fish, we're fishing for moonlight. Everything around you is making sounds, fall asleep with us by the fire. • Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1 • Format: stereo, static shot

Real field recording, no music, no AI.

Q: Is this real or AI-generated? A: Real field recording from central Poland, captured on a Zoom F3 with Rode NT1 mics. No music, no AI.

Q: Can I sleep with this on all night? A: It's a continuous ten-hour capture from a single overnight session. Density doesn't drop off toward morning.

Not on any map I've seen. That's usually a good sign.
