Early Spring Birdsong at an Old Farmstead, Western Poland

Afternoon recording from a historical parkland in western Poland. Birds layering over each other, leaves moving between the calls. Captured at a centuries-old farmstead close to the German border — early spring, quiet borderland.

Several species calling at once as the light comes through budding trees. The leaves are just starting. It sounds like a place that hasn't changed much. No traffic, no music — just the park as it was that afternoon.

Works well for long listening, background sound while working or studying, or just having something real on in the room.
