Rain Sounds for Sleeping • Black Screen • 9H

Rain sounds for sleeping with a black screen. Steady rain falling on a tin roof, recorded through the night — no interruptions, no fade, just continuous rain.

Recorded inside a tin roof garage surrounded by meadows. Two microphones captured the rain: a Rode NT1 and a Sennheiser MKE600, with the directional mic aimed at the exact point where water hits the ground. You hear the full texture of rain striking metal and splashing below.
