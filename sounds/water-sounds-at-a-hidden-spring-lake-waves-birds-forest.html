Water Sounds at a Hidden Spring Lake • Waves, Birds, Forest

Water sounds for relaxation recorded at a small hidden lake surrounded by early-spring forest. Sunny afternoon, still air. Waves lapping against a partially submerged tree on the opposite shore, waterfowl and forest birds in the background.

A last-minute Sunday call — skip the TV, pack the gear, figure out the destination on the way. We found this lake mid-walk. No plan, no map. The main camera battery was dead, so we shot on the iPhone instead.

Waves hitting the submerged branches across the water, waterfowl in the middle distance, songbirds closer in. The forest has a quieting effect that's hard to describe — we understood each other without words. Gear: Rode NT1, Zoom F3, iPhone camera.

This is a father-son project. Real places, quick decisions, no filters, no noise reduction, no AI. Recorded together with my son, who is on the autism spectrum.
