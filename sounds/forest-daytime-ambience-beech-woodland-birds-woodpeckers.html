Forest Daytime Ambience • Beech Woodland Birds & Woodpeckers

Bird song recorded at midday inside a large beech forest, summer. Woodpeckers, ravens, and rustling leaves — no wind on the mic, no background noise.

What the mic picked up first was the woodpecker. Then the ravens, somewhere deeper in the trees. Beech trees everywhere as far as the eye can see — young and old, the trunks running straight up and out of frame in every direction. I camped here for 2 days in warm weather, hammock strung between two of those trunks, just recording and sitting still. Two days alone, just nature. No traffic, no voices. We left no trace, as if we were never here. At night I heard wolves somewhere in the distance — something to keep in mind when we come back together.

Gear: Zoom F3 — static shot, mics placed on either side of the frame.
