Summer Forest Road — Bees, Birds & Butterflies

A quiet break from screens. Sit at the edge of a forest road where wild meadow flowers grow, and let the summer carry on around you.

Birds call from the trees through the morning; bees and bumblebees work the flowers; butterflies pass close to the microphone. The recording stays gentle throughout — a background for reading, a short nap, or slow work. No music, no edits.
