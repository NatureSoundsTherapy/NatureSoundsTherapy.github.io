Bird Sounds in a Beech Forest • Summer Afternoon, Near the City

Bird sounds recorded on a sunny summer afternoon in a mixed forest with old beech trees — city nearby, birds apparently unbothered. Insects buzzing throughout, leaves rustling, distant traffic audible if you listen for it.

The beech trunks here are wide and smooth, floor covered in last year's leaves. My son asked, "Dad, what's that buzzing?" I said, "Well, that's exactly what we're trying to figure out." Camera, microphone, action.

Dominant sound is bird song — layered, active, no gaps. Insects run underneath the whole recording, no wind on the mic. The traffic crept in from the edge; the birds didn't mind it.

Gear: smartphone, Rode NT1, Zoom F3 — mic placed behind the camera. This is our journey — real places, real memories. Pure sound from this place, no filters, the way we heard it.
