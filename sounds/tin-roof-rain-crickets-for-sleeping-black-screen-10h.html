Tin Roof Rain & Crickets for Sleeping • Black Screen • 10H

Rain sounds for sleeping — black screen, 10 hours. Heavy rain drops strike a tin roof while crickets fill the meadow. No music, no interruptions — just run it overnight.

Recorded from an old tin shed at the edge of a summer meadow — three nights sleeping there, with the owner's permission. Turned out to be a surprisingly decent place to bivouac. Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Sennheiser MKE600. You hear large rain drops hitting tin and earth, and dense cricket song — no birds.

Real field recording. No music, no AI. Screen off, let it run.
