Bird Sounds & a Stream Cascade for Sleep • Wetland Meadow at Sunset

For falling asleep or a quiet evening — steady birdsong over a low stream cascade in a wetland meadow at sunset, even from start to finish with nothing sudden. Recorded so you can just let it run.

We were heading home late in the afternoon and, like we always do, turned off the road to find a new place. A stream cascade sat right in the center of the meadow, wetlands stretching for kilometers in every direction. I left most of the gear in the car, pulled the shotgun mic out, and pointed it at the cascade — birds singing from every direction filled in the rest. It gets really cold once the sun drops, but we stayed through sunset anyway. My son was with me on this one.

Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Sennheiser MKE 600, Lumix S5. Static camera. No plugin touched this audio — what you hear is what was there.
