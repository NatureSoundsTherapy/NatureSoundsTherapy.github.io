Morning Forest Birdsong — Distant Cuckoo in 360° VR

Small birds sing across the canopy while a cuckoo calls from somewhere deeper in the forest. A quiet morning recording in 360° VR — no traffic, no wind, no human activity in the background.

The foreground stays close, clear bird voices in the mid and high range; the cuckoo (Cuculus canorus) sits further back, giving the scene a real sense of distance. Headphones help the spatial layout come through. Works well for reading, focus, or just having a calm forest around you for a while.
