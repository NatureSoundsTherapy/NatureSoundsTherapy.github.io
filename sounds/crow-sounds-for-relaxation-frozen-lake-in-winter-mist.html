Crow Sounds for Relaxation • Frozen Lake in Winter Mist

Crow bird sounds recorded at a frozen lake shore on an overcast winter afternoon. Mist sitting flat on the water, crows calling overhead. No wind on the mic, no people, nothing else.

Minus 10. The lake was starting to freeze — you can see the first thin ice discs drifting near the reeds in the shot. I parked the bus on the shore and set the mic up behind the camera. Static shot, didn't move it once. The crows were somewhere up in the mist; you hear them but never quite see where they're coming from. Winter has something about it that's hard to name. My son was asleep in the back — good thing we had the Webasto running.

Gear: Panasonic Lumix S5, Rode NT1, Zoom F3, Sennheiser MKE600 — static wide shot, frozen lake shore, dry reeds in the foreground.

Pure sound from this place, no filters — the way we heard it standing there. A father-son project. Recorded together with my son, who is on the autism spectrum.
