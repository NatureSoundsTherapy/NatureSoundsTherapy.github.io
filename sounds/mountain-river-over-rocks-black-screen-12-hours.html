Mountain River Over Rocks • Black Screen • 12 Hours

Mountain river white noise for sleep — twelve hours of steady water over rock, black screen the whole way, running all night with no loops, no edits, no sudden changes in volume.

Recorded in stereo at a mountain river on a summer evening with a Zoom F3 and Rode MKE600, the shotgun mic pointed straight at the water for the full twelve hours. This stretch of river can rise over 1.5 meters in an hour after rain, so we set camp nearby and kept an eye on the level through the night.

The black screen keeps your room dark and lets you turn the display off without interrupting playback. Leave it running overnight or during any rest period.

No music, no loops, no synthetic processing — just real water pushing through real rock for twelve hours.
