Summer Rain in a Forest • 360° VR • Polish-German Border

A summer rain shower in a forest on the Polish-German border, filmed in 360° VR. Put on a headset, look up through the canopy, look around at the wet trunks and the path — or just watch flat on a phone and drag with your finger to look around.

The camera and the mic sat under a tree while the rain came down. You hear drops hitting leaves above you, drops hitting the forest floor a few meters out, and the slow drip from branches that catch water before it falls. Steady, mid-weight rain — not a thunderstorm, not a drizzle.

- Location: forest near the Polish-German border, summer - Camera: Insta360 Pro (360° VR) - Recorder: Zoom F3 (32-bit float) - Mic: Rode NT1, placed under a tree
