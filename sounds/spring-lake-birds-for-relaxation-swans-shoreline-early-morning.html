Spring Lake Birds for Relaxation • Swans & Shoreline, Early Morning

Calm morning background for relaxing or a slow start — early spring birdsong by a lake, swans coming down onto the water, wind moving through bare treetops, the air still cold from winter.

Nothing added — just what was there that morning. The mic was just there; that's the whole production. No music, no effects — an outdoor field recording from early spring.
