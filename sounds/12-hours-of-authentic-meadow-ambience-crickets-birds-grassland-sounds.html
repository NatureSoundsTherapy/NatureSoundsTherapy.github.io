12 Hours of Authentic Meadow Ambience • Crickets, Birds & Grassland Sounds

Twelve hours of crickets, birds, and meadow ambience with a black screen. Recorded across kilometers of open grassland in late summer — a steady, room-filling mix for sleep or focus.

Captured over four days living out of a campervan in endless meadows. Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Rode MKE600 — the shotgun mic pointed toward the meadow while the NT1 picked up the wide backdrop. Late afternoon, stereo, nothing added.

The black screen keeps your room dark. Works overnight, with the screen off, or behind a sleep timer. No music, no loops, no edits to force a repeat — one real field recording from start to finish.
