Woodpecker Calls at Sunset, Havel River, Berlin

Woodpecker calls and bird song recorded at sunset on the Havel River near Berlin, early spring, clear sky. No water noise, no ambient interference — just birds.

A fallen bare-branched tree reaches out over still water, the sunset reflected flat across the surface. I stopped here after a full day of cycling and set the mic on the bank directly above the water. The place was empty — no other people, just steady bird activity as the light dropped. One of the most beautiful spots I've found along the Havel; I'll definitely be back.

Gear: Zoom H1N + shotgun microphone, GoPro 10 for video. We record real places on real days — no filters, no noise reduction, no AI.
