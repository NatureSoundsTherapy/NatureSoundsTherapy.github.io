Waterfowl Sounds at a Forest Lake • Early Spring Morning

Birdsong recording from a forest lake on a clear early spring morning. Ducks and other waterfowl on the water, woodpeckers tapping in the trees behind. No music, no loops.

The lake stands motionless in the early light — the far shore just a dark line of trees in the mist, the surface mirroring the sky. We set up the camera and microphones on the shore, then walked away and left it running for three hours. First signs of spring: jacket stayed in the car, ants already moving on the anthill nearby.

Birds are active from the first minute. Ducks glide across without much noise, woodpecker tapping comes and goes in the mid-distance. No wind on the mic, no traffic.

Gear: Insta360 PRO, Rode NT1, Zoom F3 — 360 VR, you can look around the full shoreline.

Recorded together with my son, who is on the autism spectrum. We're guests in this place — no filters, no noise reduction, no AI.
