Summer Wheat Field — Crickets & Warm Wind

A long, steady backdrop for quiet evenings, light reading, or sleep — recorded in an open wheat field at the height of summer.

Thousands of crickets fill the background in a continuous chorus while wind moves across the grain in slow waves, shifting the texture without breaking it. The field is wide and exposed, so the sound stays open the whole way through, and the volume holds level for overnight playback. No music, no voices.
