Cracking Ice & Winter Stillness for Focus • Frozen Lake, 2 Hours

A minimal, open background for deep focus or writing — a fully frozen lake in deep winter stillness, broken only by the odd bird and the crack of shifting ice across long stretches of near-silence. Airy rather than dense, closer to quiet nature than white noise.

Around minus twenty and no snow yet — the lake frozen solid, just hard cold and a low hum across the ice I still can't quite place. I went out for two days alone, deep into the middle of a huge beech forest; my son stayed home for this one. I've come to this same spot in summer, and in winter it looks completely different — quiet, bare, almost ominous. The mic just sat there for two hours. No music, no voices.
