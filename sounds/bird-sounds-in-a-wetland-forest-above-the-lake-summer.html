Bird Sounds in a Wetland Forest Above the Lake • Summer

Bird sounds recorded deep in a wetland forest above a lake on a sunny summer afternoon. A cuckoo, woodpeckers, insects, and steady leaf rustle — no wind on the mic, no traffic, no human noise.

Alder trunks stand in still, dark water here — the kind of spot that doesn't show up on any trail map. I'd always wondered what was further in toward the lake, so I went alone to find out. Real expedition. I'm glad I have forest experience. Once I got there: a place where no one arrives, only natural environment remains.

Dominant sound is bird song — layered, close, continuous. A cuckoo calling mid-distance, woodpecker tapping in the background, insects steady throughout. • Gear: GoPro 10, Rode NT1, Zoom F3

I always leave the forest as I found it — I respect it. No filters, no noise reduction, no AI. Recorded together with my son, who is on the autism spectrum — though this one I scouted alone. I thought this place wouldn't work for him. I was wrong.
