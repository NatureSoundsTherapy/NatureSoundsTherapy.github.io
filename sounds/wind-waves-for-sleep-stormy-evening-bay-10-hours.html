Wind & Waves for Sleep • Stormy Evening Bay • 10 Hours

A stormy evening over the bay — wind tears across the cliff and heavy waves crash below, the kind of weather some people sleep best to. Ten hours, no breaks, no music.

The wind carries most of the recording; waves rise and fall underneath it, some gusts hitting harder than others. It works as natural white noise — covering room noise, traffic, or tinnitus — and the level stays even the whole way, so you can leave it running overnight.

Gear: Sennheiser MKE 600 in a RODE BLIMP, Zoom F3, RODE NT1 for background. Our own original, unmodified field recording.
