Tin Roof Rain Sounds for Sleeping • Black Screen • 8H

Rain sounds for sleeping — black screen, 8 hours. Steady rain strikes a tin roof while crickets call through the night. Let it run and drift off.

Recorded inside a tin roof garage at night. Two microphones captured the session simultaneously — a Rode NT1 and a Sennheiser MKE600 aimed directionally at the exact point where water hits the ground. You hear rain on the roof, rain on the ground, and distant crickets underneath.

Real field recording — no music, no AI, no edits. Turn your screen off and let it run all night.
