Forest Sounds That Don't Stop | Remote Summer River Floodplain

Sleep timer — stops the video after:

Forest sounds for stress relief, plus bird sounds carrying across a summer river floodplain. Nature sounds only, no music, no narration, held at one level.

Clear summer afternoon, hot. Getting in meant a 5 km off-trail bushwhack through forest with a full backpack of gear to reach a floodplain visible only on a map, no path, no prior visit. Clouds of mosquitoes and every other flying thing you can imagine, and reeds and fallen trees to climb over most of the way. Camera set once and left alone, and past the trees a dog barking somewhere far off. Despite the brutal approach, I'm coming back — all four seasons, just to see what happens.

• Gear: Panasonic Lumix S5, Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Sennheiser MKE600 • Format: static shot, MKE600 aimed at the water, NT1 running as background

Q: Is this real or AI-generated? A: Real field recording from a river floodplain reached on foot, captured with a Zoom F3, a Sennheiser MKE600 and a Rode NT1. No music, no AI.

Q: When is this best to listen to? A: It's 2 hours and 23 minutes long, so it fits an afternoon of work or the stretch of evening before bed. Not long enough to cover a whole night.
