Nature Sounds for Sleeping | No Loops by a Wild Bieszczady River

Sleep timer — stops the video after:

Nature sounds for sleeping, held at one level with nothing that jumps out as you settle in. Water sounds from a Bieszczady mountain river, no music, no cuts.

In summer you can walk right along this river. After rain it turns into a monster. Three days camped on the bank and I didn't see another person the whole time, just the water, rocky banks grown over with forest, and the odds of a bear. No plan at all. We come back to Bieszczady whenever we possibly can.

• Gear: Panasonic Lumix S5, Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Sennheiser MKE 600 • Format: static shot, MKE 600 aimed into the water, NT1 holding the background

Listen on headphones if you can. There's a lot happening in the sides.

Q: Is this real or AI-generated? A: Real field recording from Bieszczady, captured with a Panasonic Lumix S5, a Zoom F3, a Rode NT1 and a Sennheiser MKE 600. No music, no AI.

Q: Can I sleep with this on all night? A: This one isn't a full-night file, so treat it as something to fall asleep to or nap with. Start it when you get into bed and let it run out.
