Winter Lake Reeds & Evening Birds • Quiet Nature Soundscape

Frozen lakeshore in winter. Dry reeds shifting in a light wind, distant birds calling less and less as evening comes in. Nothing else.

The lake is fully iced over. Tall reeds line the edge, moving softly when the wind catches them; bird calls drift in from the forest — sparse, unhurried, fading as the light drops. The kind of quiet you only get at remote lakes in the cold months.

No music, no added layers. Just the shore as it was. Good for sleep, wind-down, or background sound during quiet evenings.
