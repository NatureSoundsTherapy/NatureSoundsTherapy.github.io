Wind and Waves • Open Coast Ambience

Steady wind rolls across the shoreline while waves break in uneven sets. The water carries most of the low end; wind handles the rest.

No music, no talking — just the coast as it sounded that day. Runs evenly across the full length, so it works as background for sleep, reading, or long quiet hours.
