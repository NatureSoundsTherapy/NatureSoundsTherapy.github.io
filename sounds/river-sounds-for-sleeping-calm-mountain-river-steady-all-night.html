River Sounds for Sleeping | Calm Mountain River Steady All Night

Sleep timer — stops the video after:

Water sounds for sleep, nature sounds from a calm mountain river held at one level with no music, no cuts, no volume jumps. Just moving water, nothing added.

Summer, early afternoon, warm and dry. No mosquitoes. Good. One solo day trip into the Polish mountains, no crew, and I keep coming back for the empty spots. The red rocks and the water — I think it's some kind of algae, and by the end of the day that whole stretch had gone red.

• Gear: Zoom F3, Sennheiser MKE 600 • Format: static shot, mic placed off-camera on the bank

Q: Is this real or AI-generated? A: Real field recording from the Polish mountains, made with a Zoom F3 and a Sennheiser MKE 600, no music, no AI.

Q: Can I sleep with this on all night? A: Yes, that's what it's for. The river sits at one steady level, so there's no jump or fade that'll pull you back awake.

Just the place and the recorder.
