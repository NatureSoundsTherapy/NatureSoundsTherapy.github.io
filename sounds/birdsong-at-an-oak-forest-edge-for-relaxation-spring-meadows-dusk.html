Birdsong at an Oak Forest Edge for Relaxation • Spring Meadows, Dusk

Calm birdsong for relaxing or unwinding in the evening — clean, layered song at the edge of an oak forest ringed by open meadows, a sunny spring evening with no wind and nothing in the background.

We were heading back when we found this spot, memory cards nearly full and the day getting late. Quick decision: we stayed until sunset to see what the evening would bring. The birds sang from every direction, clear and steady. The meadows looked well-kept — you can tell someone tends this land — and a rooster from the farm down the road kept checking in.

Mic placed at the forest edge, next to the camera. Static shot. This is our journey — real places, real memories. We record what we find: no filters, no noise reduction, no AI.
