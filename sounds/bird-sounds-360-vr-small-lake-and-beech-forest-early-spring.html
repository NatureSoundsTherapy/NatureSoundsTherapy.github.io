Bird Sounds 360 VR • Small Lake and Beech Forest, Early Spring

Morning bird sounds recorded in 360 VR from a small lake hidden inside a beech forest — bare branches, cold air, no snow on the ground.

Cold morning. No frost, but you could see your breath. We stumbled onto this place by accident. And then I noticed the whole slope on the right — beavers had taken down every tree. The mic sat somewhere in the trees; the camera was on a small wooden dock at the edge of the lake, the one spot where you could see the whole thing at once. There's this weird hum here — I still can't fully explain it. Somewhere between insects, wind, and leaves, but none of those exactly.

Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Insta360 Pro — 360 VR, static dock.

Found by accident. Worth returning to.
