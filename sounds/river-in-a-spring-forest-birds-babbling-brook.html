River in a Spring Forest • Birds & Babbling Brook

Water moves over stones in a steady babbling pattern as a small river winds through a green spring forest, birds calling from the surrounding trees. No music, no voices.

The brook stays constant throughout; bird activity shifts naturally — sometimes close, sometimes farther into the canopy. Good for long playback in the background.

Gear: RODE NT1, Sennheiser MKE 600, Zoom F3, Lumix S5 — recorded on location with no added layers.
