Remote Forest Cabin — Deep Woodland Wind

A cabin sits far from any road, surrounded by tall trees and steady mountain wind. The forest stays dark. No traffic, no voices, no signal.

Wind moves through the canopy in long slow passes, branches creak now and then, rain arrives in patches, never heavy. Good for long sleep playback or quiet nights when the room feels too still.
