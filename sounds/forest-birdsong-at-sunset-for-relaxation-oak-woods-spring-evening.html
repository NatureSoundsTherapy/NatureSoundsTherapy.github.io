Forest Birdsong at Sunset for Relaxation • Oak Woods, Spring Evening

Spring bird sounds for relaxing or unwinding in the evening — birdsong coming from every direction at once, layered over an oak forest ringed by open meadows at sunset.

That's really the only way to describe this spot: birds overhead on every side, insects filling in the lower register, the low sun cutting through the tree line and throwing long light across the grass when we hit record. Late spring, recorded outside as the evening came on. My son and I sat on the hood of the Vitara and just listened — one of our favorite places we've found in a long time.

Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1 matched pair (stereo), handmade windshields, Lumix S5. No plugins, no AI — exactly what we heard standing there.

Recorded together with my son, who is on the autism spectrum. Everything here is a real location, captured the way we actually heard it.
