Summer Wetland Birdsong • Lush Marsh Forest at Peak Season

Deep inside a wooded bog in mid-summer, birds dominate the soundscape. Continuous song, sharp calls, and quieter chatter come from every direction. Some voices sit close. Others answer from further into the marsh.

The ground is soft and overgrown, with hidden water under the vegetation. No wind events, no human noise, no music. Just a living wetland forest doing what it does at this time of year.

Recorded as a single continuous take in wide stereo.
