Morning Birdsong for Focus • Old Polish Farmstead, Quiet Park

Morning bird sounds for relaxing or light focus — birdsong drifting in from a centuries-old park beside a big abandoned farmstead on the Polish–German border.

We came across the place by accident — a huge old estate well past its glory days, the brick building half-ruined and wrapped in ivy, with an old park around it and a lake nearby. The birds have made it their own; they call from the park the whole morning. Mic pointed into the park, static shot, recorder running. Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Lumix S5. No music, no edits between layers.

It really makes an impression — once full of life, now a ruin with the birds living in it. Recorded with my son, who is on the autism spectrum.
