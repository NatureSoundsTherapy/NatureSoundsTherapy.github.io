Birds, Crickets & Insects at Sunset — Baltic Coast, Poland • 1.5 Hours

Late spring evening near the Baltic coast in Poland, recorded from the edge of an open field as the sun went down. Birds throughout, crickets coming in quietly, and cuckoos calling every so often in the background. 1.5 hours, no music, no mid-roll ads.

Zoom F3 with a Rode NT1, mic positioned about 100 meters from the tree line facing the open field. We were sitting on a stone pile at the field's edge — that's exactly where the gear was set up too. The video is real, the audio is real. No stock footage, no AI, no filters on the sound.

Recorded together with my son, who is on the autism spectrum. A father-son project; nature is steady when days aren't.
