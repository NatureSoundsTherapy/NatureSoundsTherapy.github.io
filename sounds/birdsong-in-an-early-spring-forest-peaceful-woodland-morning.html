Birdsong in an Early Spring Forest • Peaceful Woodland Morning

A morning in an early spring forest — when the birds are back and active, but the trees are still mostly bare. Real field recording, no music, no narration, no added layers.

Early spring sounds different from late spring. The canopy hasn't filled in yet, so birdsong carries further and you hear individual birds more clearly than in the dense chorus of May or June. The forest floor is dry leaves from last autumn with the first green pushing through. Quieter overall, but more defined.

What you hear: songbirds spread out through the trees, a few closer to the mic, others further off. Occasional rustle from the leaf litter, light wind in bare branches. No traffic, no planes, no human noise.

- Location: deciduous forest, western Poland - Recorder: Zoom F3 (32-bit float) - Mic: Rode NT1 - Season: early spring, before full leaf-out

If you've listened to summer forest recordings and found them too busy, early spring is the opposite end — sparser, with more space between sounds.
