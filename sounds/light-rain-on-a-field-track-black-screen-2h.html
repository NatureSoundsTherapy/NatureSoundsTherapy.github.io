Light Rain on a Field Track • Black Screen • 2H

Two hours of soft rain on a dirt track through wet meadows — black screen, no interruptions, built for sleep.

Recorded on a field track between wet meadows in the Polish lowlands. Light, steady rain: drops on dirt and gravel close to the mic, drops on tall grass on either side, slower drips from willow leaves further back. Overcast, no wind, no thunder.

Screen stays black for the full two hours — no mid-roll ads, no sudden changes in volume. Works with the screen off. Real field recording: no music, no loops, no added layers.
