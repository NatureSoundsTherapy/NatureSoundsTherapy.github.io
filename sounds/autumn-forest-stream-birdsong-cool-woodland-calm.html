Autumn Forest Stream • Birdsong & Cool Woodland Calm

A narrow stream moves through fallen leaves in an autumn forest, light birdsong drifting between bare trees. One hour, recorded as it is.

The water sits low and steady; birds come and go. Nothing competes for attention, which makes it sit well behind reading, studying or quiet desk work. No music, no voices, no edits.
