Red Rock River Sounds for Sleeping • Black Screen • 10H

Sleep timer — stops the video after:

River Sounds for sleeping, black screen, 10 hours of calm mountain water to drift off to. No visuals, no music — just the river running from the first minute until morning.

This was recorded on a calm mountain river in high summer, in the middle of a warm day, with the microphone set low over the water where the current slides between boulders. The sound sits in a steady middle range: a broad, even rush with softer trickling lines closer to the stones, no sudden peaks, nothing that pulls you back out of sleep once you have settled.

One small thing from that session stayed with me. Towards the end of the day the water and the rocks along the bank turned a deep red — I suspect algae, warmed through by weeks of summer sun. You cannot hear colour, of course, but the river you are listening to here is that same red-stone stretch, recorded the same afternoon.

Captured with a Zoom F3 and a Sennheiser MKE 600. Real field recording — no music, no AI.

Screen off, let it run overnight.

Q: Can I sleep with this on all night? A: Yes — the file runs a full 10 hours, so it covers a whole night without you having to touch anything. The image stays completely black, so nothing lights up the room.
