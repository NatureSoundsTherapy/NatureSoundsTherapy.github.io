Forest River & Birdsong for Focus • Bieszczady Stream

Bird sounds and a quiet forest river for relaxing or light focus — birdsong over a wide, shallow stream running through the Bieszczady mountains in summer.

We were driving around Poland and kept finding places like this — we'd pull over for a minute and end up staying much longer. A wide shallow river, forest on both sides, mountains around. Birds carry it, with the water running underneath over the shallow bed. Shotgun on the current, a Rode NT1 off-frame for the room; static shot. Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Sennheiser MKE600, Lumix S5. No music, no edits between layers.

It's the quiet that gets you — nobody around, barely any traffic on the roads out here. Recorded with my son, somewhere in the Bieszczady.
