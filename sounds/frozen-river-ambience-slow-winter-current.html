Frozen River Ambience — Slow Winter Current

A steady winter river for long listening — cold and slow under a thin crust of ice, water moving beneath and breaking through in places.

The current is the main sound, with occasional cracks from the surface ice. No birds, no wind gusts, nothing else competing. Runs clean for overnight playback.
