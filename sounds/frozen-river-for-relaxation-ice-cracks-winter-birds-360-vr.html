Frozen River for Relaxation • Ice Cracks & Winter Birds • 360° VR

A calm winter background for relaxing, reading, or a slow afternoon — a wide frozen river in western Poland, drifting slush ice grinding and cracking across the surface while distant birds carry through the cold air, captured in 360° VR.

The river moves slowly under the ice; frazil and thin floes rub and pop at uneven intervals, breaking the silence without ever turning sharp. A clear, sunny winter day — nothing dramatic, even from start to finish. No music, no voices, just the river and the cold. Move the view around and listen.
