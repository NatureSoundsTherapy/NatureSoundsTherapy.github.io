Bird Sounds and Nonstop Crickets for Sleeping • Meadow by the Forest

Sleep timer — stops the video after:

📍 near Lubań🗓 summer, clear🎙 Lumix S5, Zoom F3, MKE 600, NT1⏱ 1 h 41 min✅ one take, no music, no AI

Bird sounds and crickets for sleeping, steady enough to fall asleep to, no sudden jumps. Nature sounds from a clear summer meadow by the forest, 1h 41min.

Clear sky. We slept in a tin garage, kept a campfire going, and left the gear running the whole time. An old fence is the only thing separating the meadow from the forest, and without it the forest would slowly start taking over the meadow. It was a beautiful few days. We'll come back in autumn and in winter, thanks to the kindness of the man who looks after this land.

• Gear: Panasonic Lumix S5, Zoom F3, Sennheiser MKE 600, Rode NT1 • Format: static shot, mic next to the camera

One continuous take. Thank you for the long conversations in the evenings!

Q: Is this real or AI-generated? A: Real field recording, made on site with the gear listed above. No music, no AI.

Q: Is this good for falling asleep? A: It's a good fit. The crickets carry a steady base under the birds, and the trees rustle now and then.

Our field kit: Panasonic Lumix S5 · Zoom F3 · Sennheiser MKE 600 · RØDE NT1
