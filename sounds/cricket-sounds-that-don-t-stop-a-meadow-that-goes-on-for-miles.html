Cricket Sounds That Don't Stop • A Meadow That Goes On for Miles

Bird sounds and a steady cricket chorus from late summer meadows — loud enough to fill a room, consistent enough to stay out of your way.

Drove out for four days into forests and endless meadows. Slept in the campervan, cooked outside. These meadows stretch for kilometers, one meadow after another, tens of kilometers. You could walk through these meadows for hours, they just keep going. I set up the MKE600 shotgun mic pointed across the water toward the far side of the meadow, with the NT1 pulling in the wider backdrop behind me. That geometry caught the cricket concert up close and the bird sounds layered across the distance. My son was with me on this one. He took over the responsibility of choosing the frame and setting up the equipment on a regular basis during the trip. Late afternoon. Big clouds stacked behind the grass line, and the crickets didn't let up.

Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Rode MKE600. Real places on real days. No filters, no noise reduction, no AI.
