Stream Sounds for Relaxation • Shallow Rocky Creek, Bieszczady

For an evening unwind — the steady wash of a shallow stream somewhere in the Bieszczady mountains, clear water running over stone with nothing else in the mix.

This is one of my solo trips out here — I come back a few times a year. The stream is shallow and stony enough to walk right through it, and the spot is far enough from anything that it stays quiet all afternoon. I set up late in the day and let it run as the light dropped.

Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1, Sennheiser MKE600, Lumix S5 — NT1 set back behind the frame, the MKE600 aimed at the current. Static shot.

Out here, away from everything, you can actually rest. If you look to the right of the frame you can spot the blimp on the mic. Bear country too, though I've never run into one yet. No music, no noise reduction, no AI.
