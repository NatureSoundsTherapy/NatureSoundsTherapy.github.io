Cold Wind at -14°C • Frozen Winter Forest

Sharp cold wind moves through a frozen forest at minus fourteen degrees. Branches stay stiff; snow holds the ground quiet between gusts.

The wind comes in long passes, then drops. No birds, no traffic — just the air working through the trees. Runs clean for background listening.
