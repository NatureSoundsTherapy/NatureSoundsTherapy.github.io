Mountain Stream for Sleep | 12 Hours Black Screen, No Music

A steady mountain stream runs without pause for twelve hours — no music, no edits, and no light from the screen. Real water moving over rock as natural white noise for sleep: even and unbroken, the kind of sound that covers traffic, neighbors, or whatever else carries through the night.

This is the black-screen version — the screen stays dark the whole way through, so nothing pulls you back out of sleep. The recording is real, captured on location right next to the water, the mic pointed straight at the current. Leave it running and let it carry you through the night.

No filters, no noise reduction, no AI — just the river.
