Winter Forest Stream • 360° VR Birdsong & Water

Bare trees, fresh snow, and a slow-moving stream through a quiet winter forest — small birds calling from the branches above the water. Ten hours, captured in 360° VR and 4K.

Look around on your phone, desktop or a VR headset; headphones bring out the spatial sound. The stream stays steady the whole way, so it plays well overnight or behind quiet work. No music, no voices — just the stream and the birds.
