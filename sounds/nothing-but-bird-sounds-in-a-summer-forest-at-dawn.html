Nothing but Bird Sounds in a Summer Forest at Dawn

Put this on when you need everything else to drop out. Songbirds, layered and constant — the kind of sound that fills a room without asking anything from you. Real bird sounds from a wooded canopy, recorded with no music and no loops.

We set the microphone, turned on the Zoom, and just walked — this time black screen, just the sound. Microphone out of frame while the screen stayed dark; no visual reference point. It's warm enough to walk around, and everything around here makes sounds. Didn't plan a route. Just moved through the trees and let the NT1 pick up whatever was happening.

Gear: Zoom F3, Rode NT1 — black screen (audio-only), walking tour.

We'll be back. Places like this are worth returning to.
