Bird Songs on a Berlin Hill Above the Havel • Spring Evening

Natural soundscape recorded on a hilltop in Berlin, overlooking the Havel River. Spring evening, warm air, birds calling from every direction. No city noise.

The bare trees frame a wide open view of the water below — you'd never guess you were inside a city. Day two of exploring Berlin by bike. After a long ride and a steep final climb, I stopped at the top to rest. Birds came from all directions; the city sounds just dropped away. Complete emptiness — no people, no traffic, nothing.

Birdsong only: multiple species, layered, no single dominant call. Gear: Zoom H1N + shotgun microphone, GoPro 10.

We're guests in this place. We record real spots on real days — no filters, no noise reduction, no AI.
