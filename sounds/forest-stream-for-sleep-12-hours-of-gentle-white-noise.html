Forest Stream for Sleep — 12 Hours of Gentle White Noise

Twelve hours of a forest stream on a black screen — even, unbroken water that covers the small sounds and lets a room go quiet. Made for sleeping straight through, though it sits just as well behind a book or a long stretch of work.

The flow never changes character: no music, no narration, no loop stitched in to fake the length. Water over rock and root, one take, all night. The screen stays black, so you can kill the display and leave it running.

One continuous 12-hour take, nothing touched.
