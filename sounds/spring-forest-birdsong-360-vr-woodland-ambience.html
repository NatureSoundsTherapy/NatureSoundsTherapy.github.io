Spring Forest Birdsong • 360° VR Woodland Ambience

A spring forest in 360°. Look in any direction — the trees, the light through the canopy, the open space between trunks. Birdsong carries through the recording from start to finish.

These are my own field recordings. Nothing added, nothing removed — no noise reduction, no AI processing. The faint hum of the forest stays in the recording because it belongs there.

Filmed in 4K. Best with headphones, even better with a VR headset — but moving the view on a phone or computer works too. Good for falling asleep, quiet sitting, or a calm background while working.
