Late Autumn Baltic Shore — Steady Wave Sound

One after another, waves roll in along the Baltic coast. The rhythm stays even — no music, no voices, just water meeting sand.

The shoreline is open and exposed; wind moves across the surface but never overtakes the waves. The background stays clean, long enough for overnight playback or quiet hours at a desk.
