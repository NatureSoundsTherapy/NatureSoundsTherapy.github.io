Meadow Cricket Sounds for Sleeping • Black Screen • 11H

Cricket Sounds for sleeping — black screen, 11 hours. Summer crickets from an open meadow, recorded at late evening. No birds, no interruptions — steady chirping through the night.

Captured in a summer meadow using a Zoom F3 and Rode NT1. The team slept three nights beside a rusted tin shed at the field's edge, by permission of the landowner. What you hear: dense, unbroken cricket song from the surrounding grass — nothing else.

Real field recording — no music, no AI. Screen off, let it run all night.
