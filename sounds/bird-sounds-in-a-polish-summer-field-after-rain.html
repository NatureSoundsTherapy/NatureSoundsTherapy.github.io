Bird Sounds in a Polish Summer Field • After Rain

Bird song recorded in an open field in Poland, summer afternoon, just after rain. Insects running underneath, a cuckoo calling in the distance. No wind on the mic, no traffic, no people.

The field runs flat to the tree line — forest on the right edge, open sky above. We walked a path out from the trees and stopped where the grass stretched as far as we could see, still wet from the rain. There's something about a place like this where you suddenly don't need anything else. Just to sit and be here.

Birds dominate the whole recording, insects fill the mid-layer, the cuckoo comes and goes. Clean sound from this spot — no filters, no noise reduction, no AI.

Gear: GoPro 10, Rode NT1, Zoom F3 — mic positioned behind the camera.

Recorded together with my son, who is on the autism spectrum. We both stood there a while after the recorder started. I keep thinking about what this field sounds like at night.
